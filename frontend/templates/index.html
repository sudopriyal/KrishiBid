Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about participating in KrishiBid auctions, payment protection, and quality guarantees.

In traditional mandis, farmers are forced to sell through licensed commission agents (adhtiyas) who take cuts from both sides and often fix prices. On KrishiBid, farmers list directly on the digital marketplace. Verified wholesale buyers compete in open, transparent countdown auctions. There are zero broker cuts, giving farmers up to 35% higher real earnings.

When a buyer's bid wins the auction, their funds are immediately deposited into a secure digital escrow account before produce is dispatched. Once the produce is collected from the farm gate or delivered to the buyer's depot and verified, escrow automatically releases the payment directly to the farmer's bank account. This eliminates payment defaults completely.

Absolutely! KrishiBid is specifically built for small and fragmented farmers. You can list lots starting as low as 10-20 kilograms or combine harvests with neighboring farmers through Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) for bulk auction lots.

Listings include multi-angle photographs, harvest dates, moisture and size parameters, and standardized quality grades (e.g. Grade A, Export, Organic). In addition, each farmer possesses a verified trust score based on previous successful deliveries and buyer satisfaction ratings.

Unlike traditional intermediaries who deduct 12% to 20% in arbitrary fees, KrishiBid charges zero commission to farmers. A nominal platform maintenance and escrow security fee (1.5%) is applied only upon successful transaction settlement, ensuring our interests are 100% aligned with your success.
